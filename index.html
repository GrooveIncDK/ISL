About ISL

A modern institute, an old body of knowledge.

ISL is a modern institute set up to accommodate the need for a better understanding in matters pertaining to the spiritual self. The objective of every human being on this small planet is to better understand themselves.

We have accumulated, over the past four thousand years, a complete, unabridged version of the knowledge of life. ISL has begun the task of organising this information into clearly understandable sections of human reasoning.

What We Stand On

Our Principles
1
“Vetitum Discepto Est!”

‘No compromise.’ Or more plainly put, ‘think for yourself.’ It is all too often the case that your own feelings or will are put second. No more.

Vetitum Discepto Est

Latin for ‘I dispute the prohibition’ — ISL's founding motto. In plain terms: question everything, including what you've been told not to question.

2
Truth, faith and belief

Our principles are our guide, both in what we do and who we are. Truth is self-honesty, faith is self-belief, and belief is letting go.

Before You Start

Frequently asked questions.

Where do I start? +
Read through the site and get a feel for what you're looking for, then either enrol in the course that sounds right or contact the institute to arrange a free, no-obligation conversation about your own needs.
What will I do? +
Each course follows the same shape: a short reading list, specific points to investigate as you read, a practical activity, and a written or practical exercise — which becomes the basis for a personal one-to-one tutorial with Benjamin.
How long will it take? +
It varies by course — durations for the new curriculum are being finalised and will appear on each course's page shortly.
How much will it cost? +
Pricing for the new curriculum is being finalised. Course pages will show a confirmed price as soon as it's set — in the meantime, get in touch and we'll let you know directly.
Is there any guarantee? +
Contact the institute directly and we're happy to talk through what to expect before you commit to anything.
