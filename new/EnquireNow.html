Foundations of Digital Literacy

Build the essential skills to navigate, communicate, and create with confidence in today's digital world.

Format: Online, Self-paced
Duration: 8 Weeks
Level: Beginner

Overview

This course offers a comprehensive introduction to the skills everyone needs to participate confidently in modern digital environments. Whether you are sending your first email, managing files, or exploring how websites work, each lesson is carefully designed to move you forward at your own pace.

Through a blend of guided lessons, hands-on exercises, and practical reading material, you will progressively build familiarity with the tools and concepts that underpin everyday digital activity. No prior experience is assumed — we start from the very beginning and build steadily toward genuine competence.

By the end of the course, you will have a solid grounding in core digital skills and the confidence to continue learning independently. You will also be well-placed to progress to subsequent courses in the pathway, which explore more specialised areas in depth.

What you'll explore

  • Navigating Devices & Operating Systems Get comfortable with desktops, laptops, and tablets — including file management, settings, and software basics.
  • The Internet & Online Safety Understand how the web works, how to recognise trustworthy sources, and how to protect your personal information.
  • Digital Communication Write clear emails, join video calls, and use messaging tools effectively and professionally.
  • Creating & Sharing Documents Produce and share text documents, spreadsheets, and presentations using common productivity tools.

Who it's for

This course is designed to be genuinely accessible to anyone who feels uncertain about digital technology. If any of the descriptions below sound like you, this is a great place to start your learning journey.

Complete Beginners

No experience required. We cover everything from switching on a device to browsing safely online.

Returning Students

Taken a break from learning? This course provides a structured refresh that fills gaps and builds new confidence.

Preparing for Later Courses

Gain the solid foundation you need before progressing to more advanced topics in the LearnPath curriculum.

How it works

  1. 1

    Structured Lessons

    Each week unlocks a new module with short, focused video lessons and interactive activities. Progress at whatever speed suits your life — lessons are yours to revisit at any time.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    One-to-One Support

    Every student has access to a dedicated tutor for personalised feedback, live chat sessions, and help whenever you feel stuck — we believe no question is too small.

  4. 4

    Community & Peer Learning

    Join our friendly discussion forum to share progress, ask questions, and celebrate wins with fellow learners from around the world. Learning together is more effective — and more enjoyable.
