Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 August 2026

The Institute of Spiritual Learning respects the privacy of students and visitors to ISLearning.org. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the rights available to individuals under applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Danish data protection legislation.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

The Institute deliberately keeps the amount of personal information collected from students to a minimum.

For student accounts and course administration, we collect:

  • name
  • age
  • email address

We may also process information generated through use of the course platform, such as:

  • course enrolment
  • course progress
  • assignment completion
  • course completion
  • information necessary to arrange the concluding tutorial

We do not require students to provide unnecessary personal information.

3. Personal Information Concerning Spiritual or Personal Matters

The courses concern personal introspection, spiritual learning, inner peace and balance in everyday life.

Students may voluntarily include personal information in assignments or communications with the Institute.

The Institute does not require students to disclose medical or psychological information.

Students should therefore avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in assignments or communications.

The courses are not designed to collect or process medical or psychological records.

Where a student voluntarily provides information that may constitute a special category of personal data under the GDPR, the Institute will process that information only where there is an appropriate legal basis under applicable data protection law.

4. How We Use Personal Data

Personal data is used only where reasonably necessary for the operation of ISLearning. This includes:

  • creating and maintaining student accounts
  • providing purchased courses
  • administering course access
  • monitoring course progress
  • recording completion of assignments
  • arranging the concluding tutorial
  • communicating with students about their course
  • administering purchases and payments
  • responding to enquiries
  • maintaining appropriate business records
  • complying with legal obligations

We do not sell student personal information.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending upon the particular processing activity, the Institute may rely upon one or more of the following legal bases under the GDPR:

Contract

Processing may be necessary to provide a course that a student has purchased and to administer the student's account and course progress.

Legal obligation

Certain information may need to be retained to comply with Danish accounting, tax or other legal requirements.

Legitimate interests

Certain limited processing may be necessary for the legitimate operation, security and administration of ISLearning, provided those interests are not overridden by the individual's rights.

Consent

Where consent is required by law, the Institute will seek it. Where processing is based on consent, the individual may withdraw that consent, although withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

6. Payment Information

Payments are made through PayPal.

The Institute does not store students' full payment-card information on its own systems.

Payment information is processed through the payment services used to complete the transaction and may be subject to the relevant payment provider's own privacy terms.

The Institute retains only the information reasonably necessary for transaction administration, accounting and legal compliance.

7. Website and Server Information

ISLearning.org is operated using infrastructure controlled by the Institute.

The Institute does not intentionally sell or provide student personal data to external data brokers or advertising networks.

Technical information may nevertheless be processed where necessary for the operation and security of the website, such as server logs, IP addresses, timestamps and technical connection information. Such information may be used to:

  • maintain website security
  • investigate technical problems
  • prevent abuse
  • detect unauthorised access
  • maintain reliable operation of the platform

8. Cookies

ISLearning.org may use technically necessary cookies or similar technologies required for:

  • account authentication
  • maintaining a student's logged-in session
  • remembering necessary platform settings
  • providing course functionality

Where non-essential cookies are introduced, the Institute will obtain any consent required by applicable law before using them.

9. Data Sharing

The Institute does not routinely share student personal data with third parties.

Personal data may be disclosed where necessary:

  • to comply with a legal obligation
  • to comply with a lawful request from a competent authority
  • to protect the security or integrity of ISLearning
  • to establish, exercise or defend legal claims
  • where the student has expressly authorised the disclosure

Payment transactions are processed through PayPal as described above.

10. International Transfers

The Institute does not intentionally transfer student personal information outside the European Economic Area except where such transfer is necessary for a service used by the Institute and permitted under applicable data protection law.

Where an international transfer occurs, the Institute will take the measures required by the GDPR to ensure that the transfer is lawful.

11. Data Retention

The Institute retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Student account information may be retained for the period necessary to administer the student's course and account.

Certain transaction, accounting and business records may need to be retained for longer periods because of Danish legal and tax requirements.

When personal information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable law.

12. Student Assignments

Assignments submitted by students may contain personal reflections or other information voluntarily provided by the student.

Assignments are used for the purpose of administering the course and conducting the concluding tutorial.

Students retain ownership of their own original assignment content.

The Institute will not publicly publish or commercially exploit a student's personal assignment submissions without separate permission.

13. Data Security

The Institute takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against:

  • unauthorised access
  • accidental loss
  • destruction
  • alteration
  • unauthorised disclosure
  • other unlawful processing

Because no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

14. Your Rights Under the GDPR

Subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations, individuals may have the right to:

  • request access to personal data held about them
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • request deletion of personal data
  • request restriction of processing
  • object to certain processing
  • request portability of certain personal data
  • withdraw consent where processing is based upon consent

Individuals also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

For individuals in Denmark, the relevant supervisory authority is the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

16. Children's Privacy

ISLearning courses are available only to persons aged 18 or over.

The Institute therefore does not intentionally provide course accounts to children or collect children's personal information for course participation.

If we become aware that a person under 18 has registered for a course, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation and delete information where appropriate and legally required.

  • the ISLearning service
  • the information collected
  • applicable law
  • security practices
  • the operation of the Institute

The current version will be published on ISLearning.org.
